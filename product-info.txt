1. WHAT IS REACH?
─────────────────

Reach is a local marketplace and task platform where everyday people post jobs
— plumbing, hairdressing, deliveries, tutoring, cleaning, errands — and nearby
service providers apply to get them done. What makes Reach different is how
money moves: every payment is settled through an on-chain escrow on Solana,
giving both sides trustless guarantees that the money is locked before work
starts and released only when the job is approved.

The blockchain is invisible to ordinary users. They pay in naira (Nigerian
currency) via card or bank transfer. A server-side relayer handles the Solana
transaction on their behalf. Providers can cash out their USDC earnings back
to a Nigerian bank account. Reach is crypto-powered but fiat-first.


2. THE PROBLEM
──────────────

In Nigeria's local service economy:

  • No specialist discovery hub: There is no go-to platform to discover verified
    local specialists near you, dispatch quick errands, or rent an extra set of
    hands on short notice.

  • Clients pay upfront and hope the provider shows up and does good work.
    If they don't, getting money back is a social battle.

  • Providers do the work and hope the client actually pays. Non-payment and
    delayed payment are endemic, especially for informal gig workers.

  • Price discovery is word-of-mouth. "How much does a plumber cost?" has no
    reliable answer.

  • Existing platforms (if any) hold funds in opaque custodial wallets with
    no on-chain transparency.

The result: a trust deficit that limits the market. People default to
personal networks instead of discovering better, cheaper, or closer providers.


3. THE SOLUTION
───────────────

Reach solves trust with technology:

  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  CLIENT posts task → funds locked in Solana escrow PDA             │
  │  PROVIDER applies → client accepts → provider gets advance (opt.)  │
  │  PROVIDER completes work → client approves → funds released        │
  │  DISPUTE? → admin mediates → custom split from escrow              │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  • Money is locked BEFORE work starts — providers have assurance.
  • Money is released ONLY on approval — clients have assurance.
  • Disputes are resolved with on-chain splits — transparent to both sides.
  • The entire flow works with naira cards/bank transfers — no wallet needed.

5. KEY FEATURES
───────────────

  MARKETPLACE
  • Post tasks with budget, location, description, and category
  • Geospatial matching (PostGIS) — find providers and jobs nearby
  • Application/proposal flow with pricing
  • Full lifecycle: Open → In Progress → Awaiting Approval → Completed

  ON-CHAIN ESCROW (Solana Anchor Program)
  • Task creation locks funds into escrow PDA
  • Provider collateral deposits and upfront advance releases
  • Dispute resolution with admin-mediated custom splits
  • Cancellation with fee deduction
  • Protocol fee vault for platform revenue

  THREE FUNDING RAILS
  • Native Solana wallet (Phantom/Solflare) — direct signing
  • Fiat (NGN) — card/bank via Obiex or FossaPay, relayer signs custodially
  • Multichain (planned) — NEAR Intents + Chain Signatures

  PROVIDER PAYOUT & OFF-RAMP
  • Direct USDC payout to provider Solana wallet
  • Naira off-ramp: USDC → NGNX → NGN bank transfer

  TREASURY
  • Batched USDC float management between Obiex and Solana relayer
  • Automated withdrawal/top-up cycle

  SERVICE LISTINGS
  • Provider business profiles and service listings
  • Search by keyword, category, location radius

  OTHER
  • In-app chat/messaging between clients and providers
  • Bidirectional reviews and ratings
  • User reporting system
  • KYC/identity verification (NIN/BVN via FossaPay)
  • Email verification and password reset (Brevo / ZeptoMail)
  • Cloudinary media uploads
  • PWA-ready (progressive web app)

8. BUSINESS MODEL & REVENUE
───────────────────────────

  Revenue stream               Rate / Amount
  ──────────────────────────    ─────────────
  Task creation fee             2.5% of escrow amount (250 BPS)
  Platform commission           5% of provider payout (500 BPS)
  Cancellation fee              10% of escrow (on creator cancellation)
  Off-ramp pass-through fee     ~₦50 per withdrawal (Obiex fee)

  All revenue flows into the on-chain protocol fee vault — transparent,
  auditable, and verifiable on Solana.


9. COMPETITIVE DIFFERENTIATORS
──────────────────────────────

  1. TRUSTLESS ESCROW FOR LOCAL SERVICES
     No other Nigerian gig platform settles payments through on-chain
     escrow. Reach eliminates the "will I get paid?" / "will I get
     scammed?" problem with cryptographic guarantees.

  2. FIAT-FIRST, CRYPTO-POWERED
     Users pay in naira. They never see a wallet address or sign a
     transaction. The blockchain is infrastructure, not UX friction.

  3. PROVIDER ACCOUNTABILITY ON-CHAIN
     Collateral deposits, debt tracking, and advance recovery are all
     enforced by the smart contract — not by terms of service.

  4. GEOSPATIAL MATCHING
     PostGIS-powered location search. "Who is nearby right now?" is a
     first-class feature, not an afterthought.

  5. MULTICHAIN ACCESS (PLANNED)
     Users on any chain (Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, NEAR, Polygon) will
     be able to access the marketplace via NEAR Intents + Chain
     Signatures, with all settlement funneling to Solana.

  6. TRANSPARENT REVENUE
     Platform fees sit in an on-chain vault. Revenue is public and
     auditable — a trust signal for investors and users alike.
